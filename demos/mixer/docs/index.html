ONE INTERFACE, TWO VIEWS
01 / PREVIEW

See the next take.

Program and Preview share a monitor with eight scene slots. Browse 256 scenes without losing sight of what is on air.

02 / TRANSITION

Control the mix by hand.

Cut directly, or move the T-bar through a fade. Stop halfway, complete the take, then reverse the bar for the next scene. The recording shows the mixer’s actual output throughout.

03 / ON AIR

Keep graphics on top.

Give each downstream key its own fade or cut rate, or let it follow ALL. Shift-click cuts a key immediately. Dirty Program includes the graphics; the clean feed omits them.

04 / NEXT

Take scenes and keys together.

Arm BKGD and keys to follow one fade, hold, T-bar move or wipe cut point. Turn BKGD off to take only the armed keys at the main Fade rate.

05 / TRANSITIONS

Fade, dip, wipe or go black.

Auto can halt and resume or complete a take. Dip through a chosen colour; scrub a ready cached wipe with the lever. FTB fades dirty Program to black while clean stays available.

06 / MONITOR

See the outputs and the load.

Resize two viewers, assign multiview slots and page AUX sources, or open the output wall. Inspect GPU, encoder, decoder, VRAM, power, CPU, thread and delivery meters.
